# Failures and Revisions

What broke, why it broke, and what changed as a result. Kept from the start, including the embarrassing ones — this is where most of the real engineering learning in the project is recorded, and it is the most valuable section of the final report.

Do not delete entries once resolved.

---

## Template

```
## F00n — <short symptom, in one line>
Date: YYYY-MM-DD · Stage: n · Subsystem: Sn · Related test: T00n

Symptom:        what was observed, precisely
Expected:       what should have happened
Likely causes:  the hypotheses considered
Tests run:      what was measured, and what it showed
Root cause:     the cause actually demonstrated, not the first plausible one
Fix:            what was changed
Revalidation:   which tests were re-run, and their new results
Prevention:     what changes in the design, process or checklist so it does not recur
Affects:        documents, decisions or files updated as a result
```
